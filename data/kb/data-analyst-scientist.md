# Data Analyst & Junior Data Scientist Career Guide (India Freshers)

## Role Overview
Data Analysts and Junior Data Scientists turn raw operational data into business decisions, dashboards, forecasts, and A/B test readouts. Freshers in India find strong demand across e-commerce, FinTech, logistics, EdTech, and consulting firms in Bengaluru, Gurugram, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Pune. Fresher pay typically starts around INR 35,000 to 85,000/month (internships INR 15,000 to 35,000/month), scaling quickly with SQL + Python + product intuition.

## Core Skills & Prerequisites
SQL is the #1 non-negotiable skill (CTEs, window functions, cohort retention, funnel conversion queries). Pair it with Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib/seaborn, scikit-learn), Excel/Google Sheets, one BI tool (Power BI or Tableau), and applied statistics (distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, A/B testing). Data Scientists additionally need supervised ML (XGBoost, regression, classification, cross-validation) and clear business communication.

## 4-Week Learning Roadmap
- **Week 1 — Business SQL & Funnel Analytics:** Solve 40 SQL analytics problems covering cohort retention, rolling averages, customer lifetime value, and deduplication. Deliverable: documented SQL analytics case study on an e-commerce or SaaS dataset.
- **Week 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis in Python:** Clean messy real-world CSVs with pandas, handle missing values and outliers, and visualize clear takeaways. Deliverable: reproducible EDA notebook with an executive summary (3 crisp recommendations, not 30 raw charts).
- **Week 3 — Interactive Dashboard & Experimentation:** Build a clean Power BI or Tableau dashboard with KPI cards, cohort filters, and drill-downs. Study A/B test sample size, p-values, and guardrail metrics. Deliverable: published dashboard link + one-page A/B test design doc.
- **Week 4 — Predictive Modeling & End-to-End Case Study:** Train a churn or demand-forecasting model with scikit-learn/XGBoost, evaluate using business-aligned metrics (ROC-AUC, precision@K, profit lift), and present findings cleanly on GitHub.

## Free Courses & Resources
- **Mode SQL Tutorial** (https://mode.com/sql-tutorial/): Practical analytics SQL used in real product companies.
- **Kaggle Learn Micro-Courses** (https://www.kaggle.com/learn): Free hands-on tracks for Pandas, SQL, Data Visualization, and Intermediate ML.
- **StatQuest with Josh Starmer** (https://statquest.org): Best visual explanations of statistics, hypothesis testing, and XGBoost.
- **Microsoft Learn — Power BI Data Analyst** (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi): Free official Power BI training.
- **DataLemur SQL Interview Questions** (https://datalemur.com): Real product-company SQL interview drills.

## Portfolio Projects & Interview Prep
Avoid Titanic or Iris notebooks. Analyze realistic Indian or open datasets (UPI/fintech transactions, e-commerce delivery SLA delays, SaaS subscription churn) and tie every metric to a concrete business action. Expect SQL live coding, guesstimates/case studies ("Swiggy orders dropped 12% in Bengaluru — how do you investigate?"), and basic probability.

## Adjacent Roles & Trade-Offs
- **Analytics Engineer:** Bridges Data Analyst and Data Engineer using SQL, dbt, and warehouse modeling (Snowflake/BigQuery); less ad-hoc slide decks, more software engineering rigor.
- **AI/ML Engineer:** Stronger software engineering and deep learning/LLM bar, higher compensation ceiling.
- **Associate Product Manager (APM):** Uses the same funnel and A/B test thinking to own product roadmaps rather than writing daily analysis pipelines.
